# Brand Positioning and Messaging

## Category — Locked

**Shopping-discovery platform**

Do not describe Maaroud primarily as a marketplace, because the transaction remains with the original brand.

## Positioning — Locked

Maaroud brings products from many Egyptian brands into one searchable destination while preserving each brand's ownership of checkout and customer relationship.

## Audience promise

Users can discover and compare products across fragmented Egyptian brand websites without needing to already know each brand.

## Merchant promise

Brands gain additional discovery and qualified traffic while retaining:

- Their website checkout.
- Payments.
- Fulfilment.
- Customer relationship.
- Store branding.

## Primary launch statement

> **معروض — اكتشف البراندات المصرية**

This is a launch-focus statement, not a permanent restriction on the catalog.

## Broad master-brand statement

> **Maaroud helps people discover products available in Egypt—starting with Egyptian brands.**

## Approved tagline direction

> **كل اللي بتدور عليه، معروض**

## Consumer explanation

> **اكتشف منتجات من البراندات المصرية واشتري مباشرة من البراند.**

## Merchant explanation

> **منتجاتك قدام عملاء أكتر، والبيع يفضل على موقعك.**

## Brand personality — Locked

- Modern Egyptian.
- Arabic-first.
- Intelligent and useful.
- Editorial and refined.
- Accessible rather than luxury-exclusive.
- Product-led rather than promotion-led.
- Trustworthy and restrained.

## Positioning principles

- Emphasize discovery, search, comparison, and convenience.
- Avoid implying that Maaroud is the seller.
- Avoid restricting the long-term brand to local-only products.
- Avoid crowded discount-marketplace language.
- Make direct purchase from the original brand visible and understandable.
