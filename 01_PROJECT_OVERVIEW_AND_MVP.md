# Project Overview and MVP

## Core concept — Locked

Maaroud aggregates products from Egyptian brands into a unified discovery experience. Users search and browse on Maaroud, then complete purchases on the original brand's website.

## Initial market focus — Locked

- Start with Egyptian online-first and independent brands.
- Begin with fashion and accessories because the category is visually discoverable and fragmented across individual brand websites and social accounts.
- Treat Egyptian brands as the entry wedge, not the permanent market boundary.
- Keep the platform and brand broad enough to later include:
  - All Egyptian retailers and manufacturers.
  - International brands sold through Egyptian stores.
  - A wider catalog of products available in Egypt.

## What Maaroud is — Locked

- Product-discovery platform.
- Search and browsing layer across many brands.
- Unified product categories, filters, brand pages, and collections.
- Redirect layer to the original merchant.
- Potential long-term shopping search engine for Egypt.

## What Maaroud is not — Locked

- Not a centralized retailer.
- Not a conventional marketplace that owns checkout.
- Does not initially process payments.
- Does not initially manage delivery, returns, inventory, or customer support for purchases.

## Initial user experience — Locked

The first product should provide:

- Product search.
- Category browsing.
- Filtering and sorting.
- Brand pages.
- Product pages or product-detail overlays.
- Original brand images and product information.
- Direct links to purchase on the merchant website.
- Saved products or favorites.
- Outbound-click tracking.
- Clear merchant attribution.

## Platform sequence — Locked

- Build a responsive web product first.
- Build the mobile app using the same backend, catalog, and design system after the core web experience is validated.
- Mobile technology remains part of the architecture from the beginning so the API and data model support both.

## Deferred AI features

The following are intentionally excluded from the initial MVP:

1. AI-generated standardized product presentation.
2. Personal virtual try-on using an uploaded user photo.

These can be tested later after the discovery product, product data, and user demand are validated.

## Core value proposition

> Find products from Egyptian brands that users would otherwise struggle to discover.
