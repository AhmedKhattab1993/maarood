# Visual Identity

## Visual direction — Locked

Maaroud should feel like a refined, neutral gallery that can contain products from many visually different brands without competing with them.

The interface should be:

- Product-first.
- Minimal and calm.
- Editorial.
- Highly legible.
- Arabic-first with polished bilingual support.
- Suitable for web, mobile, app icon, and favicon usage.

## Locked color palette

| Role | Color | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Primary brand | Maaroud Blue | `#1D4ED8` |
| Main background | Warm Ivory | `#FAF7F0` |
| Primary text | Ink Black | `#111111` |
| Borders and secondary surfaces | Stone Grey | `#E6E3DE` |
| Secondary text | Cool Grey | `#8B9099` |
| Success / availability | Success Green | `#22C55E` |
| Alert / reduction | Alert Red | `#EF4444` |
| Logo product dot | Warm Sand | `#DBC4A3` |
| Optional marketing gradient | Blue to Indigo | `#1D4ED8` → `#6366F1` |

## Approximate interface color balance

- 60% Warm Ivory.
- 20% Ink Black and typography.
- 10% Maaroud Blue.
- 10% greys and functional accents.

Products should provide most of the visual color.

## Final logo direction — Locked

### Symbol

- A clean open display-frame icon.
- A small Warm Sand product dot placed near the lower-right opening.
- The frame represents products being presented in one discovery space.
- The opening represents movement outward to the original merchant website.
- The dot represents an individual product being discovered.

### Wordmark

- Primary Arabic wordmark: **معروض**
- English spelling: **MAAROUD**
- Arabic-first hierarchy.
- Clean, geometric, contemporary, and highly legible.

### Approved logo behavior

- Primary logo: Maaroud Blue symbol with Arabic wordmark.
- Bilingual version: Arabic wordmark with MAAROUD beneath or beside it.
- Standalone symbol for app icon and favicon.
- Monochrome black and white variants.
- The beige dot may be removed in strict monochrome applications.

## Logo principles

- Keep the symbol simple and instantly recognizable.
- Do not force the Arabic letter ع into the icon.
- Arabic identity is carried primarily by the **معروض** wordmark.
- Maintain balanced spacing and consistent visual weight between icon and wordmark.

## Avoid

- Shopping carts.
- Shopping bags.
- Price tags.
- Storefront roofs.
- Location pins.
- Magnifying-glass logos.
- AI robots or sparkles.
- Pyramids, pharaonic motifs, or historical clichés.
- Decorative Arabic calligraphy.
- Gradients in the primary logo.
- Shadows, 3D effects, or excessive rounded geometry.
- Visually crowded marketplace styling.
