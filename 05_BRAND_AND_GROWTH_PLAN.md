# Brand and Growth Plan

## Growth principle — Locked

Participating brands should become Maaroud's first distribution channel. Acquire initial supply and ask each participating brand to help introduce Maaroud to its existing audience.

## Two parallel funnels

### Brand-acquisition funnel

Target brand → personal outreach → free listing → claim profile → co-marketing → traffic report → referrals

### Consumer funnel

Social content or search → Maaroud collection/category → product discovery → merchant click → save/follow → return through alerts or new collections

## Founding Brands program

Initial offer:

- Free listing.
- No commission initially.
- Checkout remains on the merchant website.
- Featured launch placement.
- Basic traffic and click reporting.
- Reusable “Find us on Maaroud” social assets.
- Priority access to future features.

The goal is to make participation low-risk and easy to accept.

## Channel priorities

### 1. Brand co-marketing

Primary early acquisition channel.

Provide each participating brand with:

- Launch post assets.
- Story templates.
- Direct links to its Maaroud page or collection.
- A clear announcement request.
- A simple traffic summary.

### 2. Organic TikTok and Instagram

Create repeatable content formats rather than expensive one-off creative production.

Approved recurring formats:

1. Products under a specific price.
2. Egyptian brands users may not know.
3. New products or brands this week.
4. Category collections and comparisons.

Content examples:

- Five Egyptian bags under a chosen budget.
- Egyptian brands selling linen shirts.
- Local alternatives to imported products.
- New brands added this week.

### 3. SEO

SEO should become a major long-term low-cost channel.

Create indexable pages for:

- Every brand.
- Every product.
- Categories and subcategories.
- Price ranges.
- Curated collections.
- Arabic and English product-search phrases.

### 4. Micro-creators

Prefer small shopping, fashion, and lifestyle creators over major influencers.

Test:

- Tracked links.
- Creator-curated collections.
- Affiliate-style compensation where appropriate.
- Collaborations involving featured brands.

### 5. Paid acquisition

Do not begin with broad awareness advertising.

After validation:

- Retarget product viewers.
- Boost only organic content that has already performed well.
- Run category-specific campaigns.
- Stop campaigns that do not produce qualified merchant clicks or repeat users.

## First 90-day outline

### Weeks 1–2

- Finalize identity, messaging, merchant pitch, and landing page.
- Create reusable social and merchant templates.
- Open waiting list and social channels.

### Weeks 3–6

- Begin targeted merchant outreach.
- Onboard the first reliable brands while testing ingestion.
- Publish brand-discovery and product-collection content.

### Weeks 7–10

- Improve catalog completeness and data quality.
- Run a closed beta using participating brands' audiences.
- Measure searches, clicks, saves, and return usage.

### Weeks 11–12

- Coordinate public launch with participating brands.
- Use creator collections and earned-media outreach.
- Focus launch traffic on dense categories rather than an empty broad catalog.

## Lean planning budget

These are internal planning limits, not vendor quotations.

| Item | Suggested range |
|---|---:|
| Identity and reusable templates | EGP 10,000–20,000 one-time, potentially reduced through in-house creation |
| Part-time content production | EGP 8,000–15,000 monthly |
| Creator collaborations | EGP 5,000–10,000 monthly |
| Marketing and analytics tools | EGP 1,000–3,000 monthly |
| Paid experiments after validation | EGP 5,000–10,000 monthly |

## Avoid initially

- Large influencer campaigns.
- Broad paid-awareness campaigns.
- Expensive agencies or launch events.
- Launching too many sparse categories.
- Charging brands before demonstrating value.
- Optimizing for follower count instead of qualified shopping activity.
